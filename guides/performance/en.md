## RAM by player count {#memory}

Jagex's numbers for a Dragonwilds dedicated server: 2GB of RAM, plus 1GB for each player.

- 2 players: 4GB of RAM
- 4 players: 6GB of RAM
- 6 players: 8GB of RAM

A Dragonwilds server holds 6 players at most, so 8GB carries a full server. Pick your plan for the largest group that plays at the same time, not for all your friends.

## Max players {#max-players}

The **Max players** field of the **Settings** tab takes 1 to 6. Set it to the number of players your plan carries by the list above. It applies at the next start.

@[open](panel:settings)

## When the RAM runs short {#out-of-memory}

A server with less RAM than its players need can crash. If yours crashes as more players join, lower **Max players** or move to a larger plan.
