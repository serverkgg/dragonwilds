## Which mods your server runs {#which}

Your server runs pak mods: a `.pak` file, often with a `.utoc` file and a `.ucas` file beside it. Get them from Nexus Mods, because Dragonwilds has no Steam Workshop.

Mods built on UE4SS don't run, because UE4SS needs a Windows server and your server runs on Linux.

@[open](panel:mods)

## Upload a mod {#upload}

1. Download the mod from its page on Nexus Mods
2. In the **Mods** tab, press **Upload a mod** and pick the zip as you downloaded it, or the `.pak` file
3. Restart your server, and the mod runs

If the mod comes with `.utoc` and `.ucas` files, upload the zip, so all its files arrive together.

## Every player needs the same mods {#players}

Every player who joins your server needs the same mods installed in their own game. Install them as each mod's page explains, and send the list of mods to your friends.

## Turn a mod off or remove it {#manage}

Every mod in the table has these buttons:

- **Disable**: your server stops running the mod, and its files stay
- **Enable**: the mod runs again
- **Remove**: deletes the mod from your server. Upload it again whenever you like

Restart your server after each change.

## After a Dragonwilds update {#after-update}

A Dragonwilds update can break some mods until their authors update them. If your server crashes after an update, disable the mods, start your server, and enable them again once their authors update them. To update a mod, remove it, then upload its new version.
